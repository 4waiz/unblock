# UNBLOCK brag video plan

**What it is:** a planner that shows which barriers stop wheelchair users reaching hospitals, schools and shops, and which repairs give back the most access for the money.
**For:** city planners and accessibility teams. **Sets it apart:** it decides *what to repair* under a budget and measures the gain on the real street network, not just routing.
**Most impressive claim (from the real footage):** one flight of stairs turns a 600 m trip into 1.7 km; one crossing repair takes hospital access from 62% to 73% of homes.
**Visual hook:** the street network flipping from red to cyan as a repair ripples out.
**Tone:** polished, with the punch of `default`: civic, warm, confident. Landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, ~21 s.
**Share caption:** "600 m to the hospital. 1.7 km by wheelchair. UNBLOCK finds the one repair that fixes it."

Footage is a screen recording of the real app (`/#app`) driven through the story. No personal data appears. Barriers and costs are made-up pilot data; the outro says so.
